About

I am an undergraduate student in the Engineering Experimental Class at the College of Software, Jilin University. My research interest is large language models — in particular LLM-based agents and the memory they depend on: how an agent records what it has seen, keeps that record consistent, and retrieves the right part of it later.

I am always happy to talk about these topics — feel free to reach out by email.

Research interests

  • Large language models. Reasoning, post-training, and evaluation.
  • LLM agents. Planning, tool use, and long-horizon tasks.
  • Agent memory. How agents store, maintain, and retrieve what they have learned.
